    Backup

    Emails a full copy of the system: the database (restorable), one spreadsheet per table and the product photos, to your HQ inbox.

    Restore from a backup

    This replaces the current data with the backup's (sales, stock, accounts, everything). A safety copy of what is there now is saved first in the server's backups folder. You will be signed out when it finishes.

    Clear data

    Permanently deletes every product, inventory, sales, delivery, material, partner and log record, plus all uploaded product photos. There is no undo, so email a backup first. User accounts, branches and pricing settings are kept.
